Assumptions

  • INR, October 2026, UTC timestamps and cutoff.
  • Platform precision is 3 decimals; bank lines are half-up rounded to 2 decimals.
  • Settlement lag is 1–2 days; weekends move to the next business day.
  • Gross settlement; fees are intentionally out of scope.
  • Full refunds only; month cut uses platform transaction date and bank settlement date.
  • Line tolerance is INR 0.01; aggregate residual is reported as rounding drift.
